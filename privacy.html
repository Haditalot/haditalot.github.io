Showtiles! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Showtiles! is a word-tile puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

Showtiles! has no accounts of its own, and we (the developer) do not collect your personal information. Your progress is backed up to your own iCloud, and daily leaderboard scores go through Apple Game Center. The free version shows adverts provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect some device and usage information to show those adverts, as explained below. You can remove the in-between-match adverts with a one-off in-app purchase.

What the game saves

Your progress (such as your level, unlocked outfits, settings and puzzles played) is saved on your own device and backed up to your own iCloud account, using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so it comes back if you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID. Only you and Apple can see this backup. It is never sent to us. If iCloud is turned off for Showtiles! on your iPhone, your progress stays only on the device.

Daily leaderboard (Apple Game Center)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the money you win when you solve the Daily Puzzle is sent to Apple Game Center for the daily leaderboard. Other players can see your Game Center nickname and that score, and the game shows the top scores, including your Game Center friends. Your Game Center nickname, profile and friends are controlled by Apple in your iPhone’s Settings > Game Center. We do not receive your Apple ID or contact details. Apple’s use of this information is covered by Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Adverts (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen advert between some matches (never during a match), and you can choose to watch an advert to double the money you banked after a match.

These adverts are provided by Google AdMob. To show adverts, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version and app version)
  • an advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking when your iPhone asks
  • approximate location based on your IP address
  • information about the adverts you see and tap, and crash or performance data

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, the game asks for your consent before personalised adverts are shown, using Google’s consent message. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > ADS > AD PRIVACY (when shown). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised adverts.

We do not receive this information ourselves. Google’s use of it is covered by Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

You can pay once to remove the full-screen adverts. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only whether you own Remove Ads, so it can be restored with RESTORE PURCHASE in Settings.

What we do not collect

Apart from your Game Center nickname on the leaderboard (handled by Apple), we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own analytics or tracking tools.

Haptics

The game uses your phone’s vibration (haptics) for small taps of feedback. This does not collect any information.

Update check

When the game starts, it reads a small file from our website (haditalot.github.io) to see whether a newer version of Showtiles! is available, so it can show an UPDATE button. Nothing about you or your game is sent. Like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may briefly log your IP address in its server logs.

Sharing your result

After you solve the Daily Puzzle or an Advent Calendar door, you can choose to tap SHARE. This opens your iPhone’s own share sheet with a picture and a short message about your result (never the answer), made on your phone, and a link to the game on the App Store. Nothing is shared unless you pick an app and send it yourself, and we never see what you share.

App Store rating

After a few wins the game may ask Apple to show its standard “Enjoying Showtiles?” rating pop-up. Apple controls this pop-up and any rating you give. We do not see who rated the game.

Contacting us

If you choose to send us feedback, the game opens your own email app. We only see what you decide to send, and we use it only to reply to you and improve the game. We do not share it with anyone.

Children

Showtiles! is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Adverts are set to be suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Future changes

If we add new features that use your information, we will update this policy before they go live. The date at the top always shows when this policy last changed.
