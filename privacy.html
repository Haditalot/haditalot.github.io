Showtiles! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Showtiles! is a word-tile puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

Showtiles! has no accounts and no sign-in, and we (the developer) do not collect your personal information. The free version shows adverts provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect some device and usage information to show those adverts, as explained below. You can remove the in-between-match adverts with a one-off in-app purchase.

What the game saves

Your progress (such as your level, unlocked outfits, settings and puzzles played) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. If you delete the app, this saved progress is deleted with it.

Adverts (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen advert between some matches (never during a match), and you can choose to watch an advert to double the money you banked after a match.

These adverts are provided by Google AdMob. To show adverts, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version and app version)
  • approximate location based on your IP address
  • information about the adverts you see and tap, and crash or performance data

The game never asks permission to track you, so your iPhone's advertising identifier is not shared.

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, the game asks for your consent before personalised adverts are shown, using Google’s consent message. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > ADS > AD PRIVACY (when shown). If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised adverts.

We do not receive this information ourselves. Google’s use of it is covered by Google’s own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

You can pay once to remove the full-screen adverts. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only whether you own Remove Ads, so it can be restored with RESTORE PURCHASE in Settings.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own analytics or tracking tools.

Haptics

The game uses your phone’s vibration (haptics) for small taps of feedback. This does not collect any information.

Contacting us

If you choose to send us feedback, the game opens your own email app. We only see what you decide to send, and we use it only to reply to you and improve the game. We do not share it with anyone.

Children

Showtiles! is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. Adverts are set to be suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Future changes

If we add features such as a leaderboard, we will update this policy before they go live. The date at the top always shows when this policy last changed.
